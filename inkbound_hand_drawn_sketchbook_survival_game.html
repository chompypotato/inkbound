Field Guide

Complete Tier I, then find each guardian shrine and sacrifice an Aether Crystal to summon its guardian. Gather supplies, craft gear, and use fires or warm equipment to survive cold nights.

  • Tier I (Flint & Wood): Craft Flint Tools, Ash Spear, Hand Torch, Campfire, Timber Walls, Storage Chest, and Field Workbench.
  • Tier II (Forged Iron): Stand beside a Field Workbench to forge Iron Tools, Tempered Blade, Yew Shortbow, and Masonry Walls.
  • Tier III (Alchemical): Venture into the Rocky Highlands & Shadow Mire for Aether Crystals to craft Alchemical Lanterns, Clockwork Snares, and Aether Beacons.

Field Tutorial

Movement

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Press Space to dash; a well-timed dash briefly avoids damage. Select hotbar items with 1-8 or the mouse wheel.

Foraging

Left-click nearby trees, rocks, reeds, and berry bushes to gather supplies. Bare hands work, but the hatchet is faster on trees and the pickaxe is faster on stone. Walk over dropped supplies to collect them.

Crafting

Press C to open the sketchbook and satchel. Craft basic gear from gathered materials. Workbenches unlock advanced recipes; a lit campfire cooks food and smelts ore. Click inventory slots to move or swap items.

Survival

Hunger drains over time. Food restores hunger; roasted meat also restores health and warmth. Night, rain, and colder biomes drain warmth. A lit campfire or warming equipment helps you recover. Dawn and dusk are short transition periods before the temperature shifts.

Combat

Face a target with the cursor and left-click to attack. Attacks have a recovery cooldown; heavier gear hits harder but still takes time between swings. Bows need arrows. Keep stamina for dashes, and use E near structures to interact.

Three Guardians

Place a Field Workbench and craft two Tier I tools or weapons to complete Tier I. Then explore the Deep Forest, Rocky Highlands, and Shadow Mire for the Briar Sovereign, Stoneback Colossus, and Mire Warden shrines. Follow each marker on the radar.

Each shrine consumes one Aether Crystal to summon its guardian. Every guardian has two telegraphed attacks. Leave the marked danger shape or dash before impact; only one guardian can be active at a time.

Defeat all three guardians to complete the expedition. The tracker records your progress.
